Personal project

Clareza

A personal web product for managing finances, from the interface to business rules and persistence.

Context

Clareza brings together accounts, income, expenses, cards, budgets, goals and investments. I built it from scratch with Next.js, React, TypeScript and Firebase.

My contribution

Product development, financial rules, Authentication and Firestore integration, CSV imports, automated tests and continuous delivery configuration.

Engineering decisions

  1. Money and installments

    Amounts use integer cents. Splitting installments preserves the total, including rounding differences. Paying a card statement reduces the account balance without counting the expense twice.

  2. Review before importing

    CSV import includes a preview, possible duplicate detection and category suggestions. Data is saved after confirmation, and suggestions can be changed.

  3. User-owned data

    Authentication identifies the user, while Firestore rules control access to their data. Transaction history uses cursor pagination and database-side filters.

Quality & limitations

Automated tests cover money rules, dates, validation and imports. Security rules are tested with the Firestore emulator. The pipeline is configured to check code, types, tests and builds before deployment.

No bank integration or offline queue. Statements support full payment only, and text search can require many reads. Goals and investments are tracked without automatically moving money between accounts.
