Personal project

Lectio Divina

An Android reading and reflection experience, with on-device history and scheduled reminders.

Context

The app retrieves daily liturgy through an API and guides the steps of Lectio Divina. Reflections and highlighted passages are stored locally.

My contribution

Developed a personal Android app with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, integrating an API, local persistence and reminders.

Engineering decisions

  1. State and interface

    MVVM and StateFlow organize screen state. Jetpack Compose builds the reading, reflection and history interfaces.

  2. Persistence and networking

    Room stores reflections and highlighted passages on the device. Retrofit retrieves liturgy from an API, keeping responsibilities separate.

  3. Reminders

    WorkManager and Android notifications support scheduled reminders. Delivery depends on permissions and system execution conditions.

Quality & limitations

The organization of data, repositories, ViewModels and screens makes the flow between retrieval, state and persistence visible in the public source.

Daily content depends on an external API. Reflections are stored on the device; this does not mean all liturgy is available offline. Reminders depend on notification permissions and Android execution conditions.
